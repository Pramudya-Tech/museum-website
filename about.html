Our Story

A Century of Excellence

Founded in 1923, our museum has been at the forefront of preserving and showcasing humanity's greatest achievements and most fascinating stories. What began as a small collection has grown into one of the world's most prestigious cultural institutions.

Today, we continue to inspire wonder and curiosity in visitors of all ages, bringing history to life through innovative exhibitions and educational programs.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To preserve, protect, and share the world's cultural heritage through engaging exhibitions and educational programs that inspire curiosity and foster understanding.

Our Vision

To be a leading cultural institution that connects people with history, art, and science in ways that inspire creativity, learning, and innovation.

Our Journey

1923

Museum founded with initial collection of historical artifacts.

1950

Major expansion and renovation of museum facilities.

1985

Introduction of educational programs and interactive exhibits.

Present

Continuing to innovate and inspire with world-class exhibitions.
